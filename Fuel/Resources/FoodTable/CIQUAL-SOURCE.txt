What ANSES additionally requires
--------------------------------

Beyond the licence, the CIQUAL download page requires that reused information
is not altered, that its sense is not distorted, and that the source *and the
version* are given. Two consequences for Fuel:

  * The citation above carries the year, which is the version.
  * Fuel multiplies these per-100 g figures by a weight to reach a portion.
    The result is Fuel's calculation, not an ANSES value, and must never be
    presented as one. The credit says where the per-100 g figures came from
    and stops there.

Fuel must also not imply that ANSES endorses it. It does not.

What was taken
--------------

From `alim_2025_11_03.xml` and `compo_2025_11_03.xml`, per food:

    alim_code                    the identifier, kept so a row is traceable
                                 back to the published table
    alim_nom_eng                 the English name
    const 328                    energy, Regulation EU No 1169/2011, kcal/100 g
    const 25000                  protein, g/100 g
    const 31000                  carbohydrate, g/100 g
    const 40000                  fat, g/100 g

`alim_nom_fr` and `const 400` (water) are read during the reduction — the
French name to index and to classify by, the water content to cross-check the
classification — and neither is shipped.

3,341 of the 3,484 published foods are here. The 143 missing have no energy
value in CIQUAL at all; they are mostly cocktails and composite dishes.

`tools/reduce-ciqual.py` in this repository produces this file from the archive
above, and says why each of those choices was made.
